How each report was placed

  1. Count each report once. The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. One id is one report (uniqueById).
  2. Place by cause, not by subject or wording. #4471 was merged on subjects (decision 0001). The cause comes from the tenant and the way the report came in (causes.ts). Every tenant writes the day first. A report sent through a booking-v3 form is hint: the form prints MM/DD/YYYY, people type month first, and the form reads it day first. Everything else is parser.
  3. A different wording is not a different problem. The desk read a few reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). They come from the same tenants and channels as the rest, so they stay under their cause, footnoted under the table.
  4. Ignore the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and people in day-first tenants often run a browser set to another locale, such as en-US. The table shows how far the tags are off.

What this changes

  • parser: the queued fix for #4471 covers these reports only.
  • hint: change the hint on booking-v3 (or make the form read what the hint says). The parser fix does not touch it.
  • Subject-only dedupe merged both. Decision 0001 stays as it is; a new decision record should replace it.
